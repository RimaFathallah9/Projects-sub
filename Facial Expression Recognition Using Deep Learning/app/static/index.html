Facial Expression Recognition

Research demo: a CNN trained on FER-2013 classifies a face image into 7 expression categories.

Privacy & limitations
  • Your image is processed in memory on this server and is not stored or logged.
  • Only upload images of yourself or people who have given you permission.
  • The result is a statistical guess about visible facial configuration. It is not a measurement of anyone's real emotion, and the model is less reliable for some faces, lighting conditions and poses.
  • Do not use the output to make decisions about people.
